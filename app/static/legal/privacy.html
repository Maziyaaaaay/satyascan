Privacy Policy

SatyaScan · Effective 27 August 2026

SatyaScan ("we", "the service") is a WhatsApp-based fact-checking assistant. You send it a claim, message, image, video, or voice note, and it replies with a verdict, a confidence score, and an explanation. This page describes what data we collect when you use it and how it's handled.

SatyaScan is currently operated by an independent individual developer, not a registered company.

What we collect

DataHow it's handled
Your WhatsApp phone numberEncrypted at rest. We also keep a one-way hash of it so we can recognize repeat conversations without storing the plaintext number in most of our systems.
Message text you sendUsed to run the fact-check, then only a one-way hash is kept by default — not the original text — so we can detect duplicates. The extracted claim and our verdict/confidence/explanation are kept as your fact-check history.
Images you sendProcessed in memory to check for AI-generation and search for prior matches, then discarded. Only a hash and a short summary (e.g. "likely AI-generated") are kept — not the image itself.
Videos you sendA single frame and the audio track are extracted for analysis (see below); the video file itself is not stored.
Voice notes / audioTranscribed to text to extract the claim, then discarded — the audio itself is not stored.
We do not sell your data. We do not store the plain content of your messages by default — only a cryptographic hash, plus the claim and result of each fact-check.

Third-party services we send data to

To produce a verdict, the text/image/audio you send is passed to the following services as part of processing. Each is bound by its own provider's privacy policy:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — the messaging channel itself; Meta processes all message delivery.
  • Google Gemini — reasons over your claim and the evidence gathered to produce a verdict.
  • Google Fact Check Tools API — searched for existing published fact-checks relevant to your claim.
  • Google Cloud Vision — used for image text extraction (OCR) and reverse image search.
  • Google Translate — used for language detection and translation (English/Malayalam).
  • Sightengine — analyzes images to estimate whether they are AI-generated.
  • Serper.dev — used to run a live web search for evidence on recent claims.
  • OpenAI (Whisper) — used to transcribe voice notes and video audio into text.

None of these providers receive your phone number in plaintext — only the message content needed to perform their specific task.

Data retention

  • Message content: kept only as a one-way hash, unless you've explicitly given consent to a longer retention period, in which case a short excerpt may also be kept.
  • Images/video/audio: discarded after processing; only a hash and analysis summary remain.
  • Fact-check history (claim, verdict, confidence, explanation): kept so you can be shown consistent results for a claim you or others have already asked about.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your data or stop processing your messages at any time by emailing mazitestsai@gmail.com. We'll act on deletion requests within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

SatyaScan is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Security

Phone numbers are encrypted at rest. Message content is not stored in plaintext by default. Access to the underlying data store is limited to the operator.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Effective" date at the top of this page will be updated.
